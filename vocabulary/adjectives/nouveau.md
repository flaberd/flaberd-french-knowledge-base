# Basic Information

## French

nouveau

## Part of Speech

Adjective

## Ukrainian

новий

## English

new

# Forms

## Masculine

nouveau; **nouvel** before a vowel or mute *h*

## Feminine

nouvelle

## Plural

nouveaux / nouvelles

# Examples

- J'ai acheté un **nouvel ordinateur**.

# Irregularities

The masculine singular becomes **nouvel** before a vowel or mute *h*. See [adjective position](../../grammar/adjective-position.md).
