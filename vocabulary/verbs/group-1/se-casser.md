# Basic Information

## French

se casser

## Part of Speech

Verb

## Ukrainian

ламати (собі щось)

## English

to break (a part of one's body)

# Forms *(if applicable)*

## Verb Group

Group 1 (regular -er, reflexive)

## Auxiliary Verb

être

---

# Conjugation *(if applicable)*

Present tense: je me casse, tu te casses, il/elle/on se casse, nous nous cassons, vous vous cassez, ils/elles se cassent.

---

# Examples

- Est-ce que vous vous êtes déjà cassé un bras ou une jambe ?

---

# Notes

Like [se laver](../group-1/se-laver.md) with "les mains", the reflexive pronoun here is an indirect object (casser **à qui** ? — à soi) and the body part (un bras / une jambe) is the direct object, so the past participle does not agree with the subject: "vous vous êtes cassé" — see [grammar/reflexive-verbs.md](../../../grammar/reflexive-verbs.md#exception-no-agreement-when-the-reflexive-pronoun-is-an-indirect-object).
