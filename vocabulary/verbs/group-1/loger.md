# Basic Information

## French

loger

## Part of Speech

Verb

## Ukrainian

зупинятися, проживати (тимчасово)

## English

to stay / to lodge (in accommodation)

# Forms *(if applicable)*

## Verb Group

Group 1 (regular -er)

---

# Conjugation *(if applicable)*

Present tense: je loge, tu loges, il/elle/on loge, nous logeons, vous logez, ils/elles logent.

---

# Irregularities *(if applicable)*

-ger verb: the nous form inserts an extra "e" (logeons, not logons) to keep the "g" soft, same pattern as [manger](manger.md#irregularities-if-applicable).

---

# Examples

- Dans quel hôtel (ou logement) tu as logé ?
